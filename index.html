Before You Come

Common Questions

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some dress up, some come casual — there's no dress code and no judgment here.

Where do I park?

Free parking is available in our lot off Buckwheat Road. Look for signs to the main church entrance.

What about my kids?

Children are always welcome in worship. Sunday School runs at 9:15am for all ages, including young children.

Will I follow along?

Our services follow a traditional Lutheran liturgy. A printed bulletin guides you through every step so you're never lost.

What about communion?

We practice Close Communion per LCMS tradition. If you have questions, please speak with a pastor beforehand — there's no pressure.

How long is the service?

About 60–75 minutes of Scripture, prayer, hymns, and a sermon rooted in God's Word.

What We Believe

A Church of the Open Bible — our faith is rooted in God's inspired and inerrant Word.

Our Foundation

Scripture, Grace, and Faith

St. Mark's Lutheran Church is affiliated with The Lutheran Church–Missouri Synod (LCMS), one of the largest Lutheran denominations in the United States. Our teachings are based on the Bible as God's inspired and inerrant Word — the sole authority for what we believe, teach, and practice.

The Three Solas

The Pillars of Lutheran Faith

📖
Scripture Alone

The Bible is God's inspired, inerrant Word — our only rule and norm for faith and life. We do not add to it or take away from it. Everything we believe and teach flows from Scripture.

🙏
Grace Alone

Salvation is entirely God's free gift. It is not earned by our good works, our effort, or our sincerity. God, in his love, reaches down to us — not the other way around.

✝️
Faith Alone

We receive God's forgiveness and eternal life through faith in Jesus Christ — his death on the cross for our sins and his resurrection from the dead. Faith is itself God's gift to us.

The Heart of Our Faith

One God in Three Persons

We believe in one God who has revealed himself in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Each person is fully God, and together they are one.

The FatherCreator of all things, who made us in his image and sustains us by his grace.
The SonJesus Christ, who took on human flesh, lived a perfect life, died for our sins, and rose again — our only Savior.
The Holy SpiritWho calls us to faith through the Word and Sacraments, and keeps us in saving faith throughout our lives.
Means of Grace

Word and Sacraments

God comes to us through concrete means — his Word and the Sacraments. These are not symbols or rituals we perform; they are gifts through which God himself delivers forgiveness, life, and salvation.

Holy BaptismThrough water and the Word, God creates and strengthens faith — for infants and adults alike. Baptism unites us with Christ's death and resurrection.
The Lord's SupperIn, with, and under bread and wine, we truly receive the body and blood of Christ for the forgiveness of sins — just as Jesus promised at the Last Supper.

Our History

Decades of faithful witness to the Gospel of Jesus Christ in Clermont County.

The Story of St. Mark's

From 16 Members to a Thriving Community

It started with a small group of faithful Lutherans who wanted to share the Good News of God's salvation in Christ. More than 65 years later, that vision has grown into a church and school family that continues to serve Milford and the surrounding communities.

January 1958
The First Service
16 faithful Lutherans in the Milford area gathered for the congregation's very first worship service — making St. Mark's the first Lutheran church in Clermont County.
February 1959
Growth and a New Home
In just over a year, the congregation grew to 103 members. Land was purchased on Buckwheat Road and a multipurpose building was constructed to serve the growing community.
1980
A New Sanctuary
A new sanctuary was built, largely through the volunteer labor of congregation members — a testament to the dedication and generosity of the St. Mark's family.
1981
Milford Miami Ministries
St. Mark's helped establish Milford Miami Ministries, a community coalition providing support and resources to neighbors in need throughout the area.
1990s
Expanding the Mission
St. Mark's Manor was constructed to provide housing for elderly community members. A Lutheran preschool was launched, planting the seeds for what would become St. Mark's Lutheran School.
2002
St. Mark's Lutheran School Opens
A dedicated school building was built and dedicated, expanding Christian education from Preschool through 8th grade and enrolling approximately 130 students from more than 30 churches and 11 school districts.
2012
School Addition Completed
A school addition was completed to accommodate growing enrollment and provide expanded facilities for students and staff.
2020 — Today
A Thriving Congregation
St. Mark's reported 600 baptized members, of whom 478 are adult communicants. Nine congregation members have gone on to enter full-time pastoral ministry.

Who We Are

A School Built on Faith

St. Mark's Lutheran School and Preschool is a Christian school owned and operated by St. Mark's congregation and located on Buckwheat Rd. in Milford, OH.

St. Mark's opened its doors in 1997 with just a few preschool students. A new school building was dedicated in 2003 — and with God's blessing, over the years the school grew and added grades. In 2011, St. Mark's held its first 8th grade graduation!

Today, chartered by the State of Ohio and a member of the National Lutheran Schools Accreditation, St. Mark's serves children from more than 30 churches and 11 school districts in the Cincinnati area.

Faith in Every Classroom

Christ Is Part of Everything We Do

From Preschool through 8th grade, every classroom begins the day with a devotion, prayer, and Jesus time. Prayer continues at lunchtime and throughout the day. Students also experience Bible lessons and songs during weekly Chapel services. Conversations about God with teachers and other students support their development as faithful, well-rounded students.

At St. Mark's Lutheran School, we are committed to preparing our students for life and for eternity!

Financial Aid Available

Ohio Ed Choice Expansion Scholarship

The Ohio Ed Choice Expansion Scholarship is an income-based program available to every family in Ohio. Roughly 85% of Ohio families qualify for the full award amount, which covers a majority of tuition costs at St. Mark's.

Families must apply annually for each K–8 student. If a family's household income is at or above 451% of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL), the scholarship award is reduced. Families at or below 200% FPL do not pay any tuition not covered by the scholarship.

"It is our goal that no student would be excluded from attending because of finances. Please reach out to us if you have questions about financial aid or payment plans."

Milford Miami Ministries

St. Mark's helped establish Milford Miami Ministries in 1981 — a community coalition providing support and resources to neighbors in need throughout the Milford area. This ministry reflects our core calling to love and serve those around us.

Stephen Ministry

Stephen Ministry is a one-to-one Christian care ministry that equips congregation members to provide emotional and spiritual support to people going through difficult times — illness, grief, job loss, divorce, and other life challenges.

Trained Stephen Ministers walk alongside those who are hurting, offering a listening ear, prayer, and consistent companionship in the name of Christ.

About Our Preschool

Faith-Filled Early Childhood

Every student is a precious gift from God. St. Mark's Preschool provides a warm, Christ-centered environment where young children are nurtured in faith while developing the social, emotional, and academic foundations they need for lifelong learning.

Our preschool serves children ages 3 and 4, with flexible scheduling options including all-day programs. Both PS3 and PS4 are led by experienced educators and feature small class sizes, Biblically-integrated learning, and enrichment activities — including Spanish instruction for 4-year-olds.

Our Curriculum

What We Teach

St. Mark's aligns its instructional approach with Ohio State standards and holds accreditation from the State of Ohio, recognizing National Lutheran Schools Accreditation. Every subject is taught through the lens of a Christian worldview, connecting learning to faith.

✝️
Bible & Religion

Faith isn't a separate class at St. Mark's — it's the foundation of everything. Students study Old and New Testament narratives, exploring God's redemptive plan from Creation through Christ's resurrection. Daily devotions, prayer, and weekly chapel services reinforce that the Bible is the inspired, inerrant Word of God and the guide for all of life.

📚
Language Arts

From phonics and handwriting in the early grades to grammar, spelling, vocabulary, and extended writing projects in upper grades, Language Arts is a cornerstone of every school year. St. Mark's also uses the Accelerated Reader incentive program — students read independently, take comprehension quizzes, and earn points toward goals, building fluency in a fun and motivating way.

➕
Mathematics

St. Mark's takes math seriously. Early grades build rock-solid arithmetic foundations. Starting in Grade 4, students are grouped by ability so each child is challenged at exactly the right level. The results speak for themselves: advanced students regularly earn high school Algebra 1 credit, and some students take Geometry in 8th grade — arriving at high school already ahead.

🌎
Social Studies

Students build a strong civic and historical foundation through the grades, exploring American history from its earliest discoveries through the Civil War, regional geography across the United States, and an in-depth look at Ohio's own history, geography, and natural resources. Each year builds on the last, giving students a well-rounded understanding of the world they live in.

🖥️
Technology

Students are individually assigned their own devices and receive hands-on technology instruction every week. Skills grow progressively: keyboarding starts in Grade 1, Microsoft Office in Grade 3, PowerPoint presentations in Grade 6, and spreadsheet skills in Grades 7–8. By 8th grade, students are digitally confident and ready for high school.

🗣️
Spanish

All students in Grades 1–8 receive Spanish instruction twice every week, covering reading, writing, speaking, and real conversational skills. Starting language learning early gives St. Mark's students a genuine multi-year head start — graduates leave with a meaningful foundation that sets them up for success in high school language courses.

🎨
Art

Weekly art classes introduce students to a wide range of mediums and techniques — from drawing and painting to sculpture and mixed media. Students explore the works of famous artists throughout history while developing their own creative voice and artistic confidence.

🎵
Music & Band

Every student receives music education with a goal of reading music fluently. In 3rd and 4th grade, students learn recorder. Beginning in Grade 5, students choose a band instrument — flute, clarinet, saxophone, trumpet, trombone, baritone, or percussion — and perform in Christmas and spring concerts. Music at St. Mark's connects faith, creativity, and community.

🏃
Physical Education

PE at St. Mark's goes beyond just running laps. Students develop gross and fine motor skills, learn the fundamentals of team sports including volleyball, basketball, football, and softball, and explore non-traditional activities like bowling and square dancing. The goal is to build healthy habits, teamwork, and a love of physical activity that lasts a lifetime.

Reading Program

Accelerated Reader

St. Mark's uses the Renaissance Accelerated Reader program to support and track independent reading growth for all students.

St. Mark's Lutheran School offers a variety of extracurricular activities and beloved school traditions that help students discover their gifts, build friendships, and grow in character.

School Traditions & Events

Annual Highlights

National Lutheran Schools Week
All Grades — Annual

A week-long celebration of Lutheran education featuring special activities, games, themed dress days, and events that build community and celebrate our Christ-centered mission.

Christmas Program
All Grades — December

A cherished annual tradition where students from Preschool through 8th grade come together to celebrate the birth of Christ through song, Scripture, and performance.

Class Trips
Various Grades

Students take meaningful field trips that connect classroom learning to real-world experiences — from local educational sites to larger excursions like the Ark Encounter.

Family Fun Night
All Families — Annual

A community evening where students, parents, and staff come together for games, food, and fellowship — strengthening the bonds that make St. Mark's feel like family.

🔬
Science Olympiad
Grades 5–8 — March

A national STEM competition with a district event held each March at Xavier University. Students compete in a wide range of science and engineering events against other schools.

Chapel
All Grades — Weekly

Students gather regularly for chapel services led by our pastors and staff. A time of worship, prayer, Scripture, and song that anchors the week in faith.

Learning Fair
All Grades — Annual

An annual showcase where students present projects and demonstrate what they've learned throughout the year. A celebration of curiosity, hard work, and growth.

Walkathon
All Grades — Annual

Our annual fundraising walkathon brings the whole school community together. Students collect pledges and walk laps to support St. Mark's school programs and resources.

Clubs & Academic Programs

Clubs

💻
Coding & Robotics Club
Grades 4–8

Students learn coding fundamentals and apply their skills to program robots. An engaging introduction to computer science and engineering thinking.

🧪
Science Club
Grades 4–8

Hands-on experiments and exploration beyond the regular curriculum. Students dive deeper into science through projects, investigations, and discovery-based learning.

Math Camp
PS–Grade 8 · First Friday in May

An annual evening event held the first Friday in May. Preschool through 6th grade participate while 7th & 8th graders serve as helpers. Students enjoy a cookout, sundae bar, and fun math games — using real math skills to calculate the cost of their meals and sundaes, with difficulty scaled by grade level. Weather permitting, the night ends with a family camp-out on the back field, followed by a math-themed breakfast in the morning. One of the most anticipated events of the year!

📷
Yearbook Club
Grades 5–8

Members learn photography and photo editing techniques while designing and producing the school yearbook page layouts for the whole school community.

Admissions 2026–2027

Enroll at St. Mark's

We'd love to welcome your family. Here's everything you need to know about joining St. Mark's Lutheran School.

Admissions

A Seven-Step Process

Our admissions process is designed to help you get to know St. Mark's — and help us get to know your family. Applications for the 2026–2027 school year open January 25th, 2026.

Step 1
Submit an Inquiry
Complete an online inquiry form to express your interest in St. Mark's Lutheran School. Our Admissions Coordinator, Rachel Bailey, will follow up with you personally to answer questions and guide you through the process.

📧 admissions@stmarksmilford.org  |  📞 (513) 575-3354
Step 2
Visit Our Campus
Experience St. Mark's for yourself. Our Open House is regularly held on the last Sunday in January, from 1:00–3:00 PM — a great opportunity to tour the school, meet faculty, and ask questions.

Individual campus visit appointments can also be arranged at any time through Rachel Bailey.
Step 3
"Be a Saint for a Day" Shadow Program
Prospective students are invited to shadow a current student for a day — experiencing classroom learning, chapel, lunch, and the community of St. Mark's firsthand. This is one of the best ways to know if St. Mark's is the right fit for your child.
Step 4
Submit Your Application
St. Mark's officially opens applications for the 2026–2027 school year beginning January 25th, 2026. A non-refundable $25 application fee is required at the time of submission. The application fee is separate from the enrollment fee charged upon acceptance.
Step 5
Admissions Committee Review
Each student's complete admissions file is reviewed by our Admissions Committee to determine the applicant's potential for success at St. Mark's. We consider academic readiness, family fit, and available space in the requested grade level.
Step 6
Financial Aid & Scholarships
St. Mark's wants every family to be able to afford a Christ-centered education. We offer:

Ohio EdChoice Expansion Scholarship — An income-based state scholarship available to all Ohio families. Roughly 85% of Ohio families qualify for the full award amount, which covers a majority of tuition.

Lutheran Scholarship Granting Organization — Additional scholarship funds available through our LCMS affiliation for qualifying families.

Contact the school office to learn more about financial aid options before completing your application.
Step 7
Auxiliary Services
St. Mark's is committed to supporting every learner. We offer reading therapy and speech/language therapy available on campus during school hours for students who need additional support.
